Dataset: The Movies Dataset
Kaggle: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/rounakbanik/the-movies-dataset

Included source CSV files:
- movies_metadata.csv
- ratings_small.csv
- keywords.csv
- links_small.csv

The project uses these four original Kaggle CSV files. The large ratings.csv and credits.csv files from the archive are not required by the implemented modules and are not bundled.
